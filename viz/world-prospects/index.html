UN's World Population Prospects

The UN's World Population Prospects 2024 confirms India has surpassed China as the world's most populous nation (2023), with a significant continental shift underway as India's growth slows and China's declines, while African nations like Nigeria and Pakistan rise, pointing to future demographic power shifting towards Africa and away from East Asia, with global population growth set to peak and eventually decline.

* UN population estimates are based on today's borders.
